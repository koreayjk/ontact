Zoom Setup — Part 2

Turn On Your Zoom App:
Add Scopes & Activate

You already created your Server-to-Server OAuth app and sent us the 3 keys. 🎉
Now do these 2 last steps so ONTACT can create your class meetings automatically. Takes about 3 minutes.

Why this matters

Your keys are correct, but the app also needs permission to create meetings and must be turned on (Activated). Without these two things, ONTACT cannot open meetings on your account — so please finish both steps below.

Before you start

  • Use a computer (not the phone app).
  • Go to marketplace.zoom.us and Sign In with your Zoom email & password.
  • Open your app: top-right Develop → Build App, or Manage → Created Apps, then click your app (e.g. ONTACT Class).

Step A — Add Scopes (permissions)

  1. Open the “Scopes” tab

    On the left menu of your app, click Scopes.

  2. Click “+ Add Scopes”

    A search box will appear.

  3. Search “meeting” and add the meeting permission

    Type meeting, then check the box that lets you create / manage meetings. Depending on your account it is named:

    • meeting:write:admin — “View and manage all user meetings”, or
    • meeting:write:meeting:admin — “Create a meeting for a user” (newer accounts)

    ✔ If you see both, add both. The important word is meeting + write.

  4. (Recommended) Search “user” and add user read

    Type user and check user:read:admin — “View users”.

  5. Save

    Click Done, then Continue (or Save) at the bottom.

💡 You do not need to re-send us the keys. The keys stay the same — you are only turning on permissions.

Step B — Activate your app

  1. Open the “Activation” tab

    On the left menu, click Activation (sometimes shown as the last step).

  2. Click “Activate your app”

    Press the Activate your app button.

  3. Check the status

    The status should now say “Activated” (green). Done! ✅

✅ When your app shows Activated and has the meeting scope, ONTACT can create your meetings automatically.

⚠️ Common mistakes (please double-check)

ProblemFix
App is not activatedDo Step B (Activation → Activate your app)
No meeting scopeDo Step A (add meeting:write:admin)
Client ID copied wrong (missing last letter)Copy the full Client ID again and re-send it. It is usually 22 characters.
Wrong app typeIt must be Server-to-Server OAuth — not “OAuth”, “SDK”, or “Webhook”.
🔎 Tip for copying keys: select the whole value by triple-clicking it, or use the Copy button next to it — don’t type it by hand, so no character is lost.

What to send us

If you had to re-copy your Client ID (or any key), please send us the corrected value. Otherwise, once your app is Activated with the meeting scope, you’re all set — no need to send anything again. Thank you! 🙌
